Brigade Group since 1986

Brigade was founded in 1986 by M. R. Jaishankar and has its headquarters in Bengaluru. Today its work spans homes, offices, retail, hospitality and industrial property, and its homes are found in four cities: Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad and Mysuru.

For a buyer, the value of a long record is not the number of years in itself, but what it leaves for you to look at. A developer that has been building since 1986 has other projects you can visit and see in daily use, which tells you more about finish and upkeep than any brochure.

What a listed developer means for a Brigade Banashankari buyer

Brigade Enterprises Limited, the company behind Brigade Group, is listed on the National Stock Exchange of India under the symbol BRIGADE and on the BSE under the code 532929. A listed company's shares trade publicly, and it reports its results and significant developments to its shareholders and the stock exchanges, so a good deal of information about the company as a whole is public.

What listing does not do is describe a single home. The terms that bind a Brigade Banashankari buyer are in the cost sheet, the payment plan and the agreement for sale for that apartment. Read those with the same care whatever the size of the developer.

Brigade Group's businesses

Brigade works across five segments:

  • Residential: apartment communities such as Brigade Banashankari.
  • Commercial: office space.
  • Retail: shopping and retail space.
  • Hospitality: hotels.
  • Industrial: industrial property.

For a homebuyer, the residential arm is the one that delivers your apartment. The other segments matter mainly as part of the picture of the company as a whole.

Brigade Banashankari in Brigade's Bengaluru homes

In Bengaluru, Brigade Autograph is on the Whitefield-Kadugodi Road and Brigade Belvedere at Budigere Cross. Brigade Banashankari is its pre-launch project in Uttarahalli, in Bengaluru South Taluk, about 1.5 km from Turahalli Forest.

The project plans 600 homes, 50 of 1 BHK, 50 of 2 BHK, 400 of 3 BHK and 100 of 4 BHK, in two towers of four basements, a ground floor and 40 floors on about 9 acres. A clubhouse in a building of ground and five floors, about 4.8 acres of landscape and 1,000 parking spaces complete the plan. Areas, prices and the timeline are announced at launch.

Checking the developer before you commit to Brigade Banashankari

  • Visit one of Brigade's other Bengaluru projects to see the finish and the upkeep for yourself.
  • Ask which company will be the seller named on the agreement for sale.
  • Read the cost sheet and the payment plan for the exact unit, not a general price range.
  • Ask how the possession timeline will be written into the agreement; it is confirmed at launch.
  • Pay only as the agreement sets out, into the account it names, and keep every receipt.

On a visit to another Brigade community, look at the things Brigade Banashankari will also depend on: the condition of the lobbies and lifts in the towers, the upkeep of the gardens and paths, and how the clubhouse is used and run. Residents are often the best guide to how maintenance works once a project is handed over.
