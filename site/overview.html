Brigade Banashankari Project Overview

Brigade Banashankari is a new apartment community by Brigade in Uttarahalli, South Bengaluru, near Banashankari, with Turahalli Forest about 1.5 km away and Uttarahalli Lake about 3.4 km. Planned on about 9 acres, it brings 600 homes in two residential towers, each with four basement levels, a ground floor and 40 floors above: 50 one-bedroom, 50 two-bedroom, 400 three-bedroom and 100 four-bedroom homes. A clubhouse in its own building, about 4.8 acres of landscaped open space and 1,000 parking spaces complete the community.

Brigade Banashankari homes and who they suit

The home mix leans firmly towards families. Of the 600 apartments, 400 are 3 BHK, two-thirds of the community, and 100 are 4 BHK. The 1 BHK and 2 BHK homes, 50 of each, are the smallest share.

  • 1 BHK, 50 homes: a first home for a single professional or a couple who want a Brigade address in South Bengaluru.
  • 2 BHK, 50 homes: room for a child, a visiting parent or a home office, with two bathrooms in the illustrative layout.
  • 3 BHK, 400 homes: the heart of the project, for families who need three bedrooms and a utility area off the kitchen.
  • 4 BHK, 100 homes: for larger or multi-generation households, with a family lounge besides the living room.

Because three-bedroom homes dominate, the community will be shaped largely by family households, and that is worth keeping in mind when you picture the gardens and the clubhouse in daily use. The floor plans page looks at each layout in detail.

Brigade Banashankari: two tall towers, a lot of open ground

Putting 600 homes into two towers of 40 floors each is the choice that frees up the land. More than half the site, about 4.8 acres, is given to landscape, with gardens, shaded walking paths and outdoor spaces for gatherings between the towers. The clubhouse stands in a building of its own, a ground floor and five floors above, rather than inside a residential tower.

The plan provides 1,000 parking spaces, and each tower has four basement levels. When the detailed plans are released, ask how many homes share each floor and each lift in your tower, since that shapes everyday life in a tall building as much as the view does. The master plan page explains how to read the site layout once it is out.

Brigade Banashankari and the Uttarahalli neighbourhood

The address is Uttarahalli Village and Hobli in Bengaluru South Taluk. For a family, the pull of this pocket is green space close to home, with Turahalli Forest and Uttarahalli Lake both a short drive away. Kengeri, on the western side, supplies a hospital, a metro station and a railway station within about 8 km.

For work, Global Village Tech Park is the closest of the listed office hubs at about 6.1 km, while Electronic City is a much longer drive at about 29 km. The location page covers distances, commutes and what to check on a visit.

Brigade Banashankari at pre-launch: what registering means

Before prices are public, developers often invite an expression of interest (EOI). An EOI tells the sales team which home type and budget you have in mind, so you hear first about the launch, the price list and the choice of units. It is not an allotment, and it does not fix a price or a particular apartment.

Registering on this website is simpler still: it is a request for information, with no payment. We do not collect payments. If an EOI amount is asked for when Brigade Banashankari opens for sale, read its terms, including whether and when it is refundable, before you pay.

Timing matters most for the scarcer home types. With only 50 homes each of 1 BHK and 2 BHK against 400 of 3 BHK, a buyer set on one of the smaller homes has the least choice of floor and position once sales open.
