Carpet area and super built-up area at Brigade Banashankari

When areas are released, check which measure each figure uses. Carpet area is the usable floor inside the apartment's walls: the rooms you actually furnish. Super built-up area adds the walls and a share of the common spaces, such as lobbies, corridors, lift and stair cores and amenity buildings, so it is always the larger number.

At Brigade Banashankari, 600 homes will share a clubhouse building of ground and five floors and the lobbies and lift cores of two 40-floor towers. Ask how those common areas are counted, ask for both figures for any home you shortlist, and compare homes on carpet area.

Brigade Banashankari 1 BHK and 2 BHK homes

With 50 of each, the smaller homes are the scarcest in the project. The 1 BHK layout places a single bedroom off a combined living and dining room, with a kitchen and a balcony. It suits a first purchase, a single professional or a couple.

The 2 BHK adds a second bedroom and a second bathroom, so a guest, a child or a parent has their own room and bathroom. It also works for a couple who need a separate room for working from home.

Because there are only 50 homes of each type across two towers, the choice of floor and facing in these types is likely to narrow quickly once sales open.

Brigade Banashankari 3 BHK homes

The 3 BHK is the core of the project: 400 of the 600 homes, two-thirds of the community. The illustrative layout pairs three bedrooms with a living and dining room, a kitchen with a utility area and a long balcony.

A utility area is a small space next to the kitchen for washing, drying and storage, which keeps the kitchen itself clear. A long balcony adds generous outdoor space to the home. With so many homes of this type, 3 BHK buyers will have the widest choice of floor and position, and the cost sheet will show how those choices change the price.

Brigade Banashankari 4 BHK homes

100 homes are 4 BHK. Beyond the fourth bedroom, the illustrative layout adds a family lounge alongside the living and dining room, a kitchen with utility and more than one balcony.

A family lounge lets a household keep a quieter, informal room for itself while the living room receives guests. That makes the 4 BHK the natural choice for larger families, multi-generation households, or a family that wants a bedroom set aside as a study.

Reading the Brigade Banashankari layouts

The 3D views on this page show arrangement, not exact sizes. When the measured plans arrive at launch, look for:

  • the carpet area of each room and of the whole home;
  • which bedrooms have attached bathrooms;
  • where the balcony and the utility sit relative to the kitchen;
  • the direction the living room and bedrooms face, and which tower and floor the plan applies to;
  • where windows and columns fall, since that decides where furniture can go.

Brigade Banashankari: choosing a floor in a 40-floor tower

Each tower has 40 floors above the ground floor, so the same layout can sit low, in the middle or high up. Higher floors generally bring more light, more breeze and wider views; lower floors are quicker to reach and less dependent on lifts. Neither is right for everyone, so decide what matters to your household before launch.

Ask at launch how many homes sit on each floor of each tower, how many lifts serve them, and whether the price changes with the floor.
