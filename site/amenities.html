The Brigade Banashankari clubhouse building

Brigade Banashankari plans its clubhouse as a separate building of a ground floor and five floors above, rather than on the lower floors of a residential tower, so the activity of the club stays outside the two towers where people live.

Clubhouse lounges are part of the plan. The detailed list of what each floor will hold is confirmed at launch, so ask for the floor-by-floor plan when it is released and check that it covers what your household will use most.

A clubhouse with a building to itself also raises practical questions worth asking at launch: whether membership is included in the price or charged separately, the opening hours, and whether residents can bring guests.

Brigade Banashankari landscape: about 4.8 acres

More than half of the roughly 9-acre site is landscape. The plan describes landscaped gardens, shaded walking paths and outdoor spaces for gatherings, laid out around and between the two towers.

Day to day, that means space to walk, sit and meet neighbours without leaving the gates. Shaded paths matter in Bengaluru's warmer months, and gathering spaces give the community somewhere to come together for festivals and celebrations. When the landscape plan is released, see how it divides the ground between quiet gardens and livelier shared spaces.

Parking at Brigade Banashankari

The plan provides 1,000 parking spaces for 600 homes, which is more spaces than homes, and each tower has four basement levels below its ground floor.

How many spaces come with each home, and whether they carry a separate charge, will be shown in the cost sheet at launch. If your household runs two cars, ask about it when you choose a unit.

Brigade Banashankari amenities beyond the gates

The project's own landscape is matched by green space outside it. Turahalli Forest, about 1.5 km away, suits longer walks than a garden path can offer, and Uttarahalli Lake is about 3.4 km away. For residents who enjoy the outdoors, the two together extend what the clubhouse and gardens provide. On a site visit, try the route from the site to the forest at the time of day you would actually use it.

Who the Brigade Banashankari amenities suit

  • Families with children, who make up much of a project where 400 of the 600 homes are 3 BHK, get open ground and paths within the gates.
  • Older residents in 4 BHK and multi-generation homes get shaded paths and lounges close to home.
  • Households that entertain get outdoor gathering spaces and clubhouse lounges.
  • People working from home get the clubhouse lounges as a change of scene from the apartment.
  • Two-car households get a plan with more parking spaces than homes.

Running costs of the Brigade Banashankari amenities

Amenities are paid for twice: once in the price, and then every month in maintenance. At Brigade Banashankari, residents' maintenance will look after a clubhouse building of ground and five floors, about 4.8 acres of gardens and paths, and the lifts and common areas of two 40-floor towers, with the cost shared across 600 homes.

Maintenance is usually charged by area, so a 4 BHK pays more than a 1 BHK. Ask at launch for the expected rate, what it covers, when it starts, and whether an advance or a deposit is collected with the final instalment.
