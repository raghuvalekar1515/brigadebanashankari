Frequently Asked Questions about “Brigade Banashankari” project.

About Brigade Banashankari

What is Brigade Banashankari?

Brigade Banashankari is a new apartment community by Brigade in Uttarahalli, South Bengaluru: 600 homes of 1, 2, 3 and 4 BHK in two towers rising to 40 floors on about 9 acres, with a clubhouse and about 4.8 acres of landscape. It is in pre-launch, so register now for priority access at launch.

How tall are the towers?

Each of the two residential towers has four basement levels, a ground floor and 40 floors above, and the plan provides 1,000 parking spaces.

Is Brigade Banashankari the name the project will launch with?

It may not be. Brigade has not yet announced what it will call its Uttarahalli project, so this website names it Brigade Banashankari, after the nearby Banashankari area. The land, the 600 homes and the address in Uttarahalli Village and Hobli are what identify it. If a different name is chosen at launch, registered buyers are told it together with the price list.

How big is the Brigade Banashankari site, and how is the land used?

The land measures 9 acres 1 gunta, usually rounded to about 9 acres. The homes are stacked in two residential towers so that their footprint stays small, and about 4.8 acres, more than half the land, is landscape. The rest carries the two towers, the clubhouse building of ground and five floors and the access to the basements. Each tower sits over four basement levels, and the plan provides 1,000 parking spaces.

What is decided about Brigade Banashankari today, and what is still to come?

The physical plan is known: about 9 acres, 600 homes of 1 to 4 BHK, two towers of four basements, ground and 40 floors, a clubhouse building, about 4.8 acres of landscape and 1,000 parking spaces. Still to come at launch are the carpet and super built-up areas, the price list, the cost sheet, the payment plan and the possession timeline. The details on this website are as of 21 October 2026.

When will Brigade Banashankari be ready for possession?

The possession timeline has not been announced. It is confirmed at launch and should then be written into the agreement for sale for your apartment. With two towers of 40 floors above four basement levels, ask at launch how construction will be phased and whether both towers are planned to finish together, and treat any date you hear before the launch documents as unconfirmed.

Are the pictures on this website of Brigade Banashankari?

Yes. They show the planned project: the two towers above a landscaped plaza, the arrival drive, a tower entrance, the garden court and lounge, and, inside a home, a living and dining room and a balcony. They are for representation, not photographs of finished buildings, as the project is at pre-launch. The 3D home layouts on the floor plans page are illustrative too.

Brigade Banashankari homes, sizes and plans

What configurations are available?

1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK and 4 BHK apartments: 50 one-bedroom, 50 two-bedroom, 400 three-bedroom and 100 four-bedroom homes. Areas will be shared at launch.

What are the carpet areas of Brigade Banashankari apartments?

They have not been released. Carpet and super built-up areas for the 1, 2, 3 and 4 BHK homes come with the measured floor plans at launch. Carpet area measures only the usable floor inside your apartment; super built-up area is larger because it also includes the walls and your share of common spaces, which here means tower lobbies, lift cores and the clubhouse building. Compare homes on carpet area.

What does the Brigade Banashankari 3 BHK include?

The illustrative 3 BHK layout has three bedrooms, a living and dining room, a kitchen with a utility area and a long balcony. It is the main home type, with 400 of the 600 apartments, so buyers of this type should have the most units to choose from. Measured plans with room sizes and bathroom details follow at launch, and the cost sheet will show how floor and position affect the price.

Which Brigade Banashankari home suits a joint or multi-generation family?

The 4 BHK, of which 100 are planned. Besides a fourth bedroom, its illustrative layout has a family lounge as well as the living and dining room, giving a large household two separate places to sit, one for visitors and one for the family. It also has a kitchen with utility and more than one balcony. Ask at launch which floors and which tower the 4 BHK homes are in.

How many 1 BHK and 2 BHK homes does Brigade Banashankari plan?

50 of each, against 400 of 3 BHK, which makes them the scarcest homes in the project. With so few units across two 40-floor towers, the choice of floor and facing in these types may narrow quickly once sales open. The 1 BHK suits a first home or a couple; the 2 BHK, with two bathrooms in its illustrative layout, suits a small family or someone who works from home.

Is a high floor or a low floor better at Brigade Banashankari?

It depends on your household. Upper floors tend to be brighter and airier with a wider outlook; lower floors are quicker to get in and out of, which some families with elderly members prefer. In towers of 40 floors the difference between the two is large, so decide before launch what matters more to you. A floor-rise charge, if there is one, will appear on the cost sheet at launch.

Do Brigade Banashankari homes have balconies?

The illustrative layouts show a balcony in every home type: a balcony in the 1 BHK and the 2 BHK, a long balcony in the 3 BHK and more than one balcony in the 4 BHK. Their exact sizes, and the direction each one faces, are shown on the measured plans released at launch. When you compare homes, ask whether balcony area is included in the area quoted on the cost sheet.

Brigade Banashankari price, payment and home loans

What is the price of Brigade Banashankari?

Prices will be announced at launch. Register your interest to receive the price list for the home you prefer first, with priority access at launch.

What will the Brigade Banashankari cost sheet show?

A cost sheet is the price breakdown for one particular apartment. Expect the base price, which is the rate multiplied by the area with the measure named, followed by items such as car parking, clubhouse charges, any floor-rise or preferred-location charges, maintenance deposits and taxes, with stamp duty and registration shown as extra. Request one for each apartment you shortlist at launch, so that you compare totals rather than rates.

Will Brigade Banashankari have a construction-linked payment plan?

The payment plan is shared with the price list at launch, so its type is not known yet. On a construction-linked plan, you pay part of the price at booking, more when the agreement for sale is signed, and the balance in stages as the work reaches set points, for example the basement levels or a number of floor slabs, with the last part due at possession. Where a choice of plans is offered, add up what each costs in total.

Can I buy a Brigade Banashankari home with a home loan?

Most buyers fund part of a new apartment with a home loan, and you can plan for one now. For a home under construction, the bank releases its share in parts as each payment stage falls due, and until the loan is fully paid out you usually pay interest only on the amount released. Lenders weigh your income, other loans and credit record. At launch, ask which banks work with the project.

Are stamp duty and registration included in the Brigade Banashankari price?

No. Stamp duty and registration are paid on top of the price of the apartment, at the rates in force when the sale deed is registered. The cost sheet at launch will also show which other charges are added. So when you budget for a 1, 2, 3 or 4 BHK here, add these charges to the figure on the price list rather than treating the list price as the full cost.

What is an expression of interest (EOI) for Brigade Banashankari?

Before a launch, developers sometimes invite an expression of interest: you state the home type and budget you want, sometimes with a token amount, and in return you hear about the launch and the choice of units early. It is not an allotment and does not fix a price. Registering on this website is different: it is a free request for information, with no payment of any kind.

What should I check before paying a token for a Brigade Banashankari home?

First the paperwork: a cost sheet for the specific apartment, naming its tower, floor, home type and area, and the payment plan with its due dates. Next, get in writing how much of the token comes back if you withdraw, and by when. Confirm what the price covers, such as parking and clubhouse charges. Then pay only into the account named in the developer’s documents and keep the receipt; this website takes no payments.

Will the Brigade Banashankari clubhouse cost extra?

That is not known yet. The clubhouse has a building of its own, a ground floor and five floors above, and the cost sheet at launch will show whether a clubhouse or membership charge is added to the price of your apartment. Ask too about its running cost, since a clubhouse building is kept up through the maintenance that residents of the 600 homes pay every month.

Brigade Banashankari location and connectivity

Where is Brigade Banashankari located?

In Uttarahalli, South Bengaluru, near Banashankari. Turahalli Forest is about 1.5 km away, Uttarahalli Lake about 3.4 km, Forum South Bengaluru about 5.9 km and the Kengeri Bus Terminal metro station about 7.4 km.

Which locality and taluk is Brigade Banashankari in?

The project is in Uttarahalli Village and Hobli, which falls in Bengaluru South Taluk, Bengaluru, Karnataka. Uttarahalli lies close to Banashankari, which gives this website its name for the project, with Kengeri towards the west. The map link on the location page opens the site’s position in Google Maps, which is the easiest way to plan a first visit.

Which schools and hospitals are near Brigade Banashankari?

Delhi Public School Bangalore South is about 7.3 km away by road and Gleneagles BGS Hospital, Kengeri, about 5.1 km. For a household with children in school, drive the route on a weekday morning before committing, since the school journey is repeated every day. Your visit is also a chance to ask about schools and clinics closer to the site in Uttarahalli.

How far is Brigade Banashankari from the main office areas?

Global Village Tech Park is the nearest of the office hubs listed, about 6.1 km by road. Electronic City is about 29 km away, which makes it a long daily drive. For other workplaces, use the map link on the location page at the hour you would actually travel, because a distance in Bengaluru says little about the time a trip takes at peak hours.

How do I reach Brigade Banashankari by metro, train or air?

The nearest metro station listed is Kengeri Bus Terminal, about 7.4 km by road, and Kengeri Railway Station is about 8 km away. Both are towards Kengeri, so the first leg of a metro or train trip is by road. Kempegowda International Airport is about 56.5 km away, so frequent flyers should allow for a long drive. Try the run to the metro station during your visit.

What is near Brigade Banashankari for shopping and weekends?

Forum South Bengaluru on Kanakapura Road, about 5.9 km away, is the shopping centre listed for the project. For weekends outdoors, Turahalli Forest is about 1.5 km away and Uttarahalli Lake about 3.4 km, both a short trip from home. Inside the gates, about 4.8 acres of gardens and shaded paths give residents space for an evening walk without leaving the community.

How does the Brigade Banashankari location compare with Vaishnavi Vana’s?

Both are near Turahalli Forest. Brigade Banashankari, in Uttarahalli, is about 1.5 km from the forest and looks towards Kengeri for its hospital, metro and railway station. Vaishnavi Vana, at Turahalli off Kanakapura Road, is beside the forest and about 2.3 km from Thalaghattapura metro station on the Green Line. The comparison page sets out both projects side by side.

Brigade Banashankari amenities and daily life

What amenities are planned?

A clubhouse in a building of its own, five floors above ground, and about 4.8 acres of landscaped open space, more than half the site, with Turahalli Forest about 1.5 km away.

What will the Brigade Banashankari clubhouse have?

The clubhouse is planned as a separate building of a ground floor and five floors above, with clubhouse lounges. Its full facility list, floor by floor, is confirmed at launch. When it is out, check that it covers what your household will use every week, whether that is a gym, a hall for functions or a space for children, and ask about opening hours and guest rules.

How will the 4.8 acres of landscape at Brigade Banashankari be used?

The plan describes landscaped gardens, shaded walking paths and outdoor spaces for gatherings, laid out between and around the two towers. Since the homes are stacked in tall towers, this open ground is where residents of all 600 homes will walk, play and meet. The detailed landscape plan, showing where each garden and gathering space sits, is released at launch.

How many parking spaces will each Brigade Banashankari home get?

The plan provides 1,000 parking spaces for 600 homes, so there are more spaces than homes, and each of the two towers has four basement levels. How many spaces go with each apartment, and whether they are charged separately, is set out in the cost sheet at launch. If your family runs two cars, raise it when you choose your home.

What will maintenance cost at Brigade Banashankari?

No rate has been given yet; it comes at launch. Expect the charge to be worked out per square foot, which means it rises with the size of your home. Ask at launch which costs it includes, from lift servicing in the 40-floor towers to garden upkeep and the running of the clubhouse, the date from which it is billed, and whether a lump sum is taken in advance at handover.

What is daily life like in a 40-floor tower at Brigade Banashankari?

Life in a tall tower depends a great deal on lifts and on how many homes share each floor. Brigade Banashankari’s towers each have four basement levels, a ground floor and 40 floors above, and the floor plates showing homes per floor are released at launch. Ask then how many lifts serve each tower, whether there is a service lift, and how power backup for the lifts is planned.

Brigade Group, the developer

Who is the developer?

Brigade Group, whose company is Brigade Enterprises Limited. It was founded in 1986 by M. R. Jaishankar, has its headquarters in Bengaluru and is listed on the NSE and the BSE. It builds homes in Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad and Mysuru. Its South Bengaluru homes include Brigade Meadows on Kanakapura Road and Brigade Komarla Heights in Uttarahalli.

Where are Brigade’s shares listed, and why does it matter to a buyer?

Brigade Enterprises Limited is listed on the National Stock Exchange of India under the symbol BRIGADE and on the BSE under the code 532929. That makes information about the company’s finances public. For your own purchase, though, rely on the documents for your apartment at Brigade Banashankari: the cost sheet, the payment plan and the agreement for sale.

Which other Brigade projects can I visit near Uttarahalli?

In South Bengaluru, Brigade’s homes include Brigade Meadows on Kanakapura Road and Brigade Komarla Heights in Uttarahalli itself. Elsewhere in the city it has Brigade Autograph on the Whitefield-Kadugodi Road and Brigade Belvedere at Budigere Cross. Where you can, visit one that is already occupied: it shows you the upkeep of lift lobbies and gardens, and residents can tell you how maintenance works after handover.

Which cities and businesses does Brigade work in?

Brigade builds homes in Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad and Mysuru; its named projects include Brigade Stellaris in Velachery, Chennai, and Brigade Barcelona in Hyderabad. Beyond housing, the group works in commercial, retail, hospitality and industrial property. For a Brigade Banashankari buyer, the residential side is what matters most, since that is the arm that will build and hand over the 600 homes in Uttarahalli.
