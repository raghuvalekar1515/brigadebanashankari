Brigade Banashankari Project Overview

Brigade Banashankari is a new apartment community by Brigade in Uttarahalli, South Bengaluru, near Banashankari, with Turahalli Forest about 1.5 km away and Uttarahalli Lake about 3.4 km. Planned on about 9 acres, it brings 600 homes in two residential towers, each with four basement levels, a ground floor and 40 floors above: 50 one-bedroom, 50 two-bedroom, 400 three-bedroom and 100 four-bedroom homes. A clubhouse in its own building, about 4.8 acres of landscaped open space and 1,000 parking spaces complete the community.
